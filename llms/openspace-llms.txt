# OpenSpace

> OpenSpace is the Visual Intelligence Platform for builders. Powered by Spatial AI, it turns smartphone, 360° camera, and drone capture into real-time jobsite intelligence that connects the field and the office for faster, more confident decisions across documentation, coordination, progress tracking, and risk management.

OpenSpace serves general contractors, trade contractors, owners, developers, and investors, with 360° capture, AI-powered field documentation, BIM coordination, progress tracking, and construction risk management.
