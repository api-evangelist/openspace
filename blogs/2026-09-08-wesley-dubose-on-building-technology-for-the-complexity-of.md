Wesley DuBose is a Product Manager at OpenSpace. Before making the move into technology, he worked across several roles in construction. He started out supporting a superintendent in the field, worked with estimating and project management teams, and was most recently a project manager for a large mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) contractor in Texas.
